# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good intent, respect different experience levels, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted disclosure of private information, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and sustained personal attacks.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.
